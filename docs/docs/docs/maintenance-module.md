Section 7 – Maintenance Incident Management Workflow
Suppose a driver reports:
"The brakes feel weak."
Maintenance Workflow
Driver identifies a vehicle fault

            ↓

Driver submits maintenance/incident report

            ↓

System logs the report and assigns a reference number

            ↓

Fleet Manager reviews the report

            ↓

Severity of the fault is assessed

            ↓

Vehicle status changes to "Pending Inspection"

            ↓

Technician is assigned

            ↓

Technician performs diagnosis

            ↓

Work order is created

            ↓

Required spare parts are identified and requested

            ↓
Vehicle repair is carried out

            ↓
Quality inspection and road test are completed

            ↓
Vehicle is marked "Roadworthy"
           ↓
Maintenance record is updated
            ↓
Fleet Manager approves completion
            ↓
Driver is notified that the vehicle is available
            ↓
Incident is closed
